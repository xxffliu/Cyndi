# Retuning after the 2026-09-16 review

All runs: full 329-molecule test set (`bench/329_test_set.mol2`), MMFF94,
`MOGA_Random_Seed 0.42`, one core of a 2-core Linux container, GCC 13 `-O2`.
Metric: minimum Kabsch-aligned heavy-atom RMSD between the input (bioactive)
conformer and the generated set, computed with `bench/rmsd_core.py`
(`numpy.linalg.eigvalsh`). Single seed throughout, so differences below about
0.02 A in the mean are noise.

Reproduce any row with:

```
cyndi -input bench/329_test_set.mol2 -output out.mol2 -parm <param file>
python bench/analyze.py bench/329_test_set.mol2 out.mol2 <label> -q
```

## Build progression (fixed settings: pop 100, gen 300, mutation 0.2)

| build | opt settings | wall time | mean | median | <=1 A | <=2 A | confs/mol |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| as-found (v2) | iter 50, grad 0.01, ls 50 | 451 s | 0.633 | 0.437 | 80.5% | 95.7% | 56.8 |
| step 1: algorithm + crash fixes | iter 50, grad 0.01, ls 50 | 340 s | 0.639 | 0.431 | 79.3% | 96.4% | 65.8 |
| step 3: optimiser + force field | iter 20, grad 0.5, ls 50 | 178 s | 0.596 | 0.365 | 79.6% | 97.0% | 68.6 |
| step 3 + line-search cap | iter 20, grad 0.5, ls 10 | 138 s | 0.596 | 0.365 | 79.6% | 97.0% | 68.6 |

The line-search cap (50 -> 10 interpolation steps) is free: identical mean,
median, <=1 A, <=2 A and the same 1520 aborted minimisations, 29% less time.

Step 1 alone buys correctness and speed, not accuracy. That is expected and
worth stating plainly: the fixed mutation operator changes *how* the search
explores, but at 700 energy evaluations per molecule the search is not the
binding constraint.

## Mutation probability (step-3 build, pop 100, gen 300)

| `MOGA_Mutation_Probability` | wall time | mean | median | <=1 A | <=2 A |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 0.05 | 126 s | 0.621 | 0.373 | 77.2% | 95.4% |
| 0.10 | 130 s | 0.624 | 0.397 | 79.3% | 96.0% |
| 0.20 | 138 s | 0.596 | 0.365 | 79.6% | 97.0% |
| 0.30 | 135 s | 0.594 | 0.361 | 79.9% | 96.4% |
| **0.40** | **131 s** | **0.586** | 0.351 | **81.5%** | **97.0%** |
| 0.60 | 136 s | 0.581 | 0.343 | 81.5% | 95.7% |

Every earlier sweep of this parameter measured nothing: `real_mutate` computed
`delta` with integer division, so `deltaq` was always 0 and the operator never
changed a gene (measured: 2 changes in 5400 gene visits on `3ert`). The only
effect of the probability was how many `randomperc()` draws it consumed --
equivalent to changing the seed.

The recommendation is **0.4**, not the textbook `1/N`. With `MOGA_SBX`'s
eta_m = 20 the periodic polynomial operator is a local perturbation of about
16 degrees on average, so a high rate behaves like a local search layered on
SBX rather than a restart. Past 0.4 the mean keeps drifting down but the <=2 A
rate starts to lose ground, which is the direction that matters for a
conformer generator.

## Search budget (step-3 build, mutation as noted)

| pop / gen | mutation | wall time | mean | median | <=1 A | <=2 A |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 100 / 300 | 0.2 | 138 s | 0.596 | 0.365 | 79.6% | 97.0% |
| 300 / 300 | 0.2 | 117 s | 0.609 | 0.385 | 79.6% | 96.0% |
| 100 / 1000 | 0.2 | 339 s | 0.521 | 0.268 | 83.6% | 97.9% |
| 100 / 1000 | 0.4 | 344 s | 0.508 | 0.260 | 81.8% | 98.5% |
| 100 / 3000 | 0.4 | 723 s | 0.479 | 0.240 | 85.4% | 97.9% |

`execuateMOGA` is steady-state: one "generation" produces two children, so the
evaluation budget is roughly `PopSize + 2 * MaxNumGen`. Population size only
sets the initial random sample and the cost of `compete()`; it is not a search
budget knob. Raising it from 100 to 300 at fixed generations made things
slightly worse *and* slightly faster (a broader initial sample fills the
epsilon archive differently and fewer conformers survive to minimisation).

Generations are the real lever, and the returns are still positive at 3000.
Recommended:

- **fast**: gen 300, mutation 0.4 -- 131 s, mean 0.586 A. Better than the
  as-found code on every metric at 3.4x the speed.
- **accurate**: gen 1000, mutation 0.4 -- 344 s, mean 0.508 A. 24% better than
  the as-found code in 25% less time.

## Not measured here

- Distance-dependent dielectric or scaled charges for the electrostatic term
  during search (review section 5). `MMFF94Ele` uses a buffered Coulomb
  potential with dielectric 1, which in vacuum over-rewards intramolecular
  salt bridges and can collapse flexible charged ligands. Worth an A/B.
- Clustering before minimisation instead of after.
- Disabling stretch/bend/stretch-bend/OOP during the MOGA phase (they are
  constant under pure torsional rotation). This needs the constant baseline
  added back into `individual::energy` so the reported total energy does not
  shift on the `MOGA_Optimize_Conformer N` path.
- Replacing the conic line search with Armijo backtracking or L-BFGS
  (`KNOWN_ISSUES.md` #5).
